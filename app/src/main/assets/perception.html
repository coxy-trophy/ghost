Clause grammar

Only the first four render unconditionally — and even those count as nothing while they read unknown.

activity=Always. Carries confidence and how long it has held.
location=Always. Needs a stored home point to say more than unknown.
speed=Above 0 km/h.
light=Always, with raw lux once measured.
proximity=Always — unless the pocket sensor already said it.
steps=Above 0 per minute.
mic=Listening, closed, or unavailable.
heard=An utterance arrived. Cut to 15 words.
camera=A frame was captured. Brightness and facing only — never content.
posture=Known.
pocket=yesOnly when true.
heading=A compass fix exists.
motion=Only when it disagrees with activity, or stands alone.
just_picked_upWithin 90 seconds.
nearby=Count above zero. Names only for already-paired devices.
call=Ringing or in a call. Idle is the boring default.
signal=Weak, or during a call.
nfc:onOnly when on.
The three rules

Silent unless it says something. The block rides on every prompt into a small model with an 8k window, so a sense that adds nothing must cost nothing. Twelve registrations, and an idle phone spends what it did with three.

The guard cannot disagree with the block. Whether to send it and what it says come from one render pass, so they cannot drift apart.

Buckets leave, values stay. A GPS fix becomes home; a stranger's phone becomes a number; a camera frame becomes a brightness. What cannot be reconstructed from the block cannot leak through it.

Deliberately not sensed
gyro / accel / magThe rotation vector is already the HAL's fusion of all three. Registering them would wake the CPU at 100 Hz to re-derive what arrives anyway.
biometricsFingerprint and face are authentication APIs, not readable sensors. There is no honest field to fill.
NFC tagsNeeds a physical tap into a foreground reader mode, which a background daemon cannot do.
continuous visionShe cannot ingest pixels. Describing a frame's contents would be inventing something and handing it over as real.
always-on listeningRemoved, not deferred — the spotter saturated on room noise. The mic opens when you open it.
